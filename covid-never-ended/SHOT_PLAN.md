# Shot plan: What If COVID-19 Never Ended?

Format: 1920x1080 landscape, 30 fps, 5:12 (about 9,360 frames).
Timings come from `voice/timeline.json` (sentence times) and `voice/words.json` (word times for captions). Narration is locked (Fenrir, v3 natural pacing).
Style: the same 2D stick-figure look as the earlier videos (flat colour, thick dark outlines, pastel backgrounds, one accent colour per section, word-by-word captions, small shakes on key words, slow push-in on every hold, cuts 2 frames before their word). Reuse the shared drawing kit and caption system from the earlier compositions.

## Guardrails for this topic (important)
- **Deaths and illness are real.** Show the death-toll numbers as quiet counters on a plain background. **No bodies, no coffins, no patients in distress, no hospital-bed close-ups, no crowds of mourners.** Keep colours muted for those lines and let the narration carry the weight.
- **No politics.** Do not draw any leader, flag, party symbol, protest scene, police or crowd conflict. The China section shows empty streets, apartment blocks with people at windows, and a barrier tape icon only.
- **No stereotyping of any country or group.** Use varied, ordinary stick figures.
- **No medical advice or claims.** Do not show syringe-heavy imagery, "cure" visuals, or anything that reads as telling viewers what to do. A neutral vial/vaccine icon on the "authorised" line only.
- **No real brand logos** (no video-call app logos, airline logos, or pharma logos). Generic icons only.
- **Balance:** every cost scene is paired with its upside or counterpoint (freedom and loneliness, telehealth workaround, "kept the best parts"). Never frame restrictions as simply right or wrong.
- **The scenario is the EMERGENCY never ending**, not the virus staying deadly. Show this with the recurring switch device (below), never with scary virus imagery.

## Recurring visual devices
- **The emergency switch:** a big wall switch labelled EMERGENCY, stuck in the ON position (a small red light). It appears in the hook and the rules, flickers under pressure in the cost sections, and is finally flipped OFF on "In May twenty twenty-three..." while a small virus icon stays beside it on "The virus didn't". It is the visual spine of the video.
- **The calendar:** a tear-off calendar. 15 days, then 45, then the pages keep flipping and never stop ("never ended"); it lands on a blank page at the end.
- **Counters:** rolling numbers on their words (about 15 million vs 5.4 million, 262 days, 25 million, 5-7% to about 1 in 4, 8 points, 1 in 1,000 to 4 in 10, 63x, about 60%, about 3%, about 25%, under 11 months, 2 Dec 2020, May 2023).
- **The screen:** a window frame the stick figures look through, for remote work, school and doctor scenes.

## Dead air rules (from the silence audit)
Whenever the narration pauses 0.7 s or longer, something on screen keeps moving (a flipping calendar page, the switch light pulsing, a counter ticking, a slow push-in), and the sound bed or a soft effect carries the pause. The two `[beat]` pauses (after "Now imagine it never ended." and "The emergency ended.") may hold a quiet frame for about 1.2 s with a slow push-in.

## Build order (Claude Code on the Mac)
1. Build sections 1-3 (0:00-1:08) as the **look test**: render 6 half-size stills and wait for approval.
2. Build the remaining sections back to back, then show 10-12 stills across them.
3. Sound mix, then a **half-resolution draft render** to check motion, then ask before the full render.
4. Full render, frame-by-frame quality check, then the SRT subtitle file.

- Every on-screen number must match `SCRIPT.md` and `FACTS.md`. Show "28 million" only if verified; otherwise write "tens of millions".
