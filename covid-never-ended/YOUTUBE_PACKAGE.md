## Description (no links; paste as is)
In March 2020, the United States launched a campaign called "Fifteen Days to Slow the Spread." Within two weeks it was extended to forty-five. What if the emergency never ended?

This video imagines a world where the Covid-19 emergency never switches off: the rules, the empty offices and the screens stay on. We look at what that would do to work, school, health care, travel and our minds, using real examples such as Melbourne's long lockdowns, China's strict rules, the jump in remote work and telehealth, and the drop in school test scores. Then we ask what actually changed in the real world, and what stayed.

This is a hypothetical thought experiment. It is not medical advice, and it does not take a side on lockdowns or vaccines. Figures come from public sources such as the World Health Organization and are rounded; some are estimates.
