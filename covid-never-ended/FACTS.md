## Sensitivity notes
- Deaths are stated respectfully; no blame, no politics, no medical advice, no claims beyond WHO/CDC-level sources.
- No statements about vaccine safety or lockdown effectiveness beyond sourced facts.
- China section is factual and neutral; avoid showing leaders or political imagery.
- The scenario is "the emergency never ended", not "the virus stayed deadly", and says so.
