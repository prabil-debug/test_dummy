## Look and structure (what it does)
- Dark cinematic illustrated style, flat puppet characters, moon and stars, warm candle palette; a small round presenter avatar top-left.
- A parchment scroll card at the top carries the Malayalam narration line (2 lines, large serif-like Malayalam, a red dot bullet); the card replaces bottom captions. A small English chip/label sits in the scene (ONE WORD, DAY 15, THE PLAN, THE MAN WHO NEVER LIED, THE BOW IS LAID DOWN, IT TOUCHED EARTH). Red stamp labels for key reveals.
- Narrative device: countdown/label stamps, a "twist" reveal, then an ethical question as the ending: two cards "FAIR? / FOUL?" with "COMMENT BELOW". End card: "Another land. Another legend." + Follow. (A series tagline.)
- Written Malayalam is dramatic storytelling register: punchy exclamations ("ഒരൊറ്റ 'ചതിവാക്ക്'!", "വീഴ്ത്തി!", "ഉറപ്പിച്ചു!"), rhetorical question at the end.

## What we can borrow for the finance page (technique, not content)
1. Continuous delivery with a bed, fewer dead gaps (our gaps are 0.7 to 0.95 s between sections).
2. A deeper, bassier narrator voice (test v3 below).
3. Stamp labels for key reveals (e.g. "ONE RULE", "DAY 15", "THE MISTAKE") and one English chip per scene.
4. A poll-style ending with two cards (e.g. "NEED? / WANT?") plus "COMMENT BELOW" instead of a teaser.
5. A series tagline on the end card.
## What not to copy
His story, characters, art, layout identity, and name; our page keeps Ramu and its own palette.

## Limits
I cannot hear the Malayalam. Slang and accent cannot be judged from measurements; the on-screen text shows a formal, literary storytelling register, not casual slang. The user must say what to take from the speech itself.
