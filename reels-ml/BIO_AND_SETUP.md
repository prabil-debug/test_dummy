## Compliance rules for the bio (important)
- Never write "financial advisor", "SEBI registered", "expert", "guaranteed", "returns", or promise income. You are not registered, and the page teaches only.
- Keep the disclaimer line. Do not link to any broker, app or insurer for now.
- Category: choose "Education" or "Digital creator". Avoid "Financial service" (implies a regulated business).
- Paid collaborations later: use Instagram's "Paid partnership" label and clear disclosure; sponsors need an email.
