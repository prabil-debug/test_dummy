## Claude's flags (written down so nothing is hidden; the user decides)
Claude built the video as asked, but recommends NOT posting these lines as they are:
1. "ലിക്വിഡ് ഫണ്ടുകൾ ... മാർക്കറ്റ് റിസ്ക് ഒട്ടുമില്ലാതെ ... സേഫ് ഫണ്ടുകൾ" (liquid funds have NO market risk, safe): inaccurate. Liquid funds are low-risk, not zero-risk, and returns are not guaranteed. Mutual funds are "subject to market risks".
2. "ആർബിട്രാജ് ഫണ്ടുകൾ ... ബാങ്ക് എഫ് ഡി പോലെ തന്നെ ഫുൾ സേഫാണ്" (arbitrage funds are as fully safe as a bank FD): inaccurate. Arbitrage funds are equity-oriented hybrid funds; there is no FD-style guarantee and short-term returns can be low or negative.
3. "ഒരുപാട് ടാക്സ് ലാഭിക്കാനും പറ്റും" (saves a lot of tax for the 30% bracket): depends on holding period and the tax rules in force; unverified here (user said skip verification).
4. Naming product categories as "two good options" for a goal reads as a recommendation: SEBI/ASCI treat investment advice by unregistered people strictly (see NICHE_FINANCE_PLAN.md). Safer: "options some people consider; ask a SEBI-registered adviser".
5. "₹50,000 to ₹42,000 in one week" (a 16% fall in a week for an equity fund) is unusually large; if shown as the user's real experience it must be true, otherwise keep the "example" tag.
6. "Golden rule" for 3 years is a commonly quoted guideline, not a rule: "പൊതുവേ പറയാറുണ്ട്" is safer.
