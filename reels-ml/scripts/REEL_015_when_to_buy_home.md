## Honest flags (narration left exactly as written, your call)
1. **"ധൈര്യമായി വാങ്ങിക്കോ" is a direct go-ahead.** "If these three are OK, buy" tells viewers to make a large purchase. Under finfluencer norms, personal advice from someone not registered is the risky area. Education version: "ഇത് മൂന്നും ഓക്കെയാണെങ്കിൽ വീട് വാങ്ങുന്നത് പരിഗണിക്കാം".
2. **"ബാക്കി കാശ് എസ്ഐപിയിൽ ഇടൂ" is an investment instruction.** It tells people to invest in SIPs, with no risk wording in the narration. The on-screen line and end card carry the risk, but the voice does not. "നിക്ഷേപിക്കാം, പക്ഷേ നഷ്ടസാധ്യതയുണ്ട്" is safer.
3. **35% is a rule of thumb, not a rule.** Commonly quoted EMI-to-income limits are 30–40%, and banks lend on their own formulas (often allowing a higher share). The script states it as "കൂടരുത്" (must not exceed). "സാധാരണ പറയുന്ന ഒരു കണക്ക്" makes that clearer.
4. **The three checks leave out several factors**: the interest rate, the total cost with maintenance and tax, job security, family needs, and whether the price is fair. "These three are enough" ("ഈ മൂന്ന് കാര്യങ്ങൾ… ചെക്ക് ചെയ്താൽ മതി") overpromises.
5. **"വാടക + SIP" is again one-sided**, as in Reel 013: it ignores rent rising every year and that returns are not guaranteed. The video does not show a promised outcome.
6. **"കഴിഞ്ഞ റീൽ എഴുനൂറോളം പേരാണ് ഷെയർ ചെയ്തത്"** is a claim about your own Reel. I could not verify it, so please check the insight number before posting. The video shows "700+ shares".
