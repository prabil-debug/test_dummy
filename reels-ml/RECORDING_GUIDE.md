# Recording guide (own voice)
1. Phone voice memo is enough. Quiet room, soft furnishings (a wardrobe or a blanket over you helps), phone about a hand-span from your mouth, slightly to the side (reduces "p" pops). No fan, AC or traffic if possible.
2. Record the whole script in ONE take, line by line, leaving about one full second of silence after each line. If you fumble, stay silent two seconds and repeat the line; say nothing else. I'll keep the best take of each line.
3. Speak at your natural, relaxed pace. Do not read like a news anchor. Smile on the hook lines.
4. Send me the audio file in this chat (m4a, mp3 or wav all work).
5. Tell me any word you changed, so captions match your speech.
6. What I will do: cut each line, remove breaths/noise, even out loudness, rebuild the video with your voice and the same mix. If a line sounds wrong to you, re-record just that line.
7. Licence note: it is your voice recorded by you; no cloning. Keep original files.
Fallback: the AI voice stays available when you cannot record.
