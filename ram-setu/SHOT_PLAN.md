# Shot plan: What If Ram Setu Still Stood Today?

Format: 1920x1080 landscape, 30 fps, 6:00 (about 10,800 frames).
Timings come from `voice/timeline.json` (sentence times) and `voice/words.json` (word times for captions). Narration is locked (Fenrir, v3 natural pacing).
Style: the same 2D stick-figure look as the internet and India videos (flat colour, thick dark outlines, pastel backgrounds, one accent colour per section, word-by-word captions, small shakes on key words, slow push-in on every hold, cuts 2 frames before their word). Reuse the shared drawing kit and caption system from `InternetFull` / `IndiaFull`.

## Guardrails for this topic (important)
- **No depiction of any deity or Ramayana character.** Do not draw Lord Ram, Hanuman, Sita or any religious figure. When the Ramayana is mentioned, show only a simple closed book or scroll icon with the text "Ramayana".
- **Treat faith and science as equals.** When the narration gives both views, show two quiet, equal-weight cards side by side (a book icon / a rock-and-wave icon). No scene should favour or mock either.
- **No religious symbols** (no temple, om, flags, saffron/tricolour palettes). The temple and pilgrimage appear only as generic "pilgrims" stick figures with bags and walking sticks, no symbols.
- **No precise national borders and no labelled disputed areas.** Draw India and Sri Lanka as soft, stylised shapes (dot-blob or rounded silhouettes), the shoals as a dotted line. Do not draw or label any maritime boundary line. Do not show Katchatheevu or any specific disputed area.
- **The 1964 cyclone is a real tragedy (an estimated ~1,800 deaths).** Show it respectfully: waves, wind lines, an empty railway track, an empty station sign, a lighthouse silhouette. **No people in distress, no bodies, no drowning figures, no dramatic gore.** A stopped train silhouette is allowed. Keep colours muted and let the narration carry the weight.
- **No real brand logos.** Generic icons only.
- **No stereotypes.** Varied, ordinary stick figures.

## Recurring visual devices
- **The dotted shoal line:** a thin dotted line between two soft land shapes. It is the visual anchor in every section (appears, breaks, fills, glows).
- **Sea level gauge:** a vertical gauge on the left for the hook (sea rising over the dry strait).
- **Counters:** rolling numbers on their words (48 km, 1 m, 424 nm, 72.5 m, 17 m, 111 km, 3.5 hours, 3,600, 55 km, 30 km, 20 billion, 23 km, $3.6B → $5B).
- **Year tags:** 1914, 2022, 2025, 1964, 2023 pop in as small date stamps.

## Dead air rules (from the silence audit)
Whenever the narration pauses 0.7 s or longer, something on screen keeps moving (push-in, drifting waves, a counter ticking, a small idle animation), and the sound bed or a soft effect carries the pause. The two `[beat]` pauses (after "Now imagine it never broke." and "The stones are still there.") may hold a still, quiet frame for about 1.2 s, but still with a slow push-in.

## Build order (Claude Code on the Mac)
