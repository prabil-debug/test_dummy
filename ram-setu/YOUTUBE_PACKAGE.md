## Description (no links; paste as is)
What if you could walk from India to Sri Lanka? Thousands of years ago, you could. Today, a chain of shallow limestone shoals about 48 kilometres long, known as Ram Setu or Adam's Bridge, is all that remains.

In this video we imagine a hypothetical: what if a continuous causeway still stood there? What would change for the sea, for ships, for trade, for nature, for the people who live nearby, and could we use it today? We also look at a real train-and-ferry route that once crossed here, and at the proposals for a modern link.

This video does not take a position on whether the structure is natural or man-made. It looks at what faith, geology and engineering each have to say, and asks a "what if".
