# Fact sheet: What If All Deserts on Earth Turned Completely Green?

## Sensitivity notes
- Nomadic and desert peoples are mentioned respectfully, with no group stereotyped and no "empty wasteland" framing.
- It is a hypothetical; the script says it won't explain where the rain comes from.
- Avoid depicting any real country's flag, leader or political imagery in the China section.
- Avoid showing deaths or distress; the "losers" are animals and a way of life, shown gently.
