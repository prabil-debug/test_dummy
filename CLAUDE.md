# Malayalam Instagram Reels (finance)

Plan: `channel-kit/REELS_MALAYALAM_BRIEF.md`. Work lives in `reels-ml/`. The English YouTube videos (other folders) are separate: ignore them for Reels.

## Audience
Malayali young earners, ~20-35. Goal: sponsorships.

## Tone
Friend-to-friend story, honest, educational, never advice. Disclaimer only on the end card and in the caption, no top tag.

## Malayalam style
- The user is a native speaker and edits every script. Keep their text; put risky claims in script notes.
- Short spoken sentences. Voice scripts spell English terms in Malayalam script; on screen, common finance words (EMI, rent, loan) show in English. Use "നിങ്ങൾ/നിങ്ങൾക്ക്". See `reels-ml/SLANG_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

## Reel format
1080×1920, 30 fps. Hook in 2 s, new shot every 2-3 s. Captions: bottom dark band, white text, gold numbers, no stroke, y≈1338-1418; content ≥60 px from sides, above y≈1250, clear of right buttons (x>920, y 870-1760). Covers: grid shows a 3:4 slice (y 240-1680), view count over its bottom: keep key content in y 300-1500, nothing top-right. No "next video" teaser unless the script has one.
Per reel: review-cut mp4, notes (flags, math check), caption pack, 3 covers.

## What has worked
- Concrete ₹ numbers in hooks and covers.
- Friend story, then numbers.
- Detailed props, calm character.

## Decisions
- Ramu is locked: 22, no mundu. No blur or glow. One focus per scene, no tiny text.
- Number scenes use dark data cards. Font: Noto Sans Malayalam + Poppins (medium/bold).
- Never reveal points before the voice says them: locked "?" cards, teaser captions and covers.
- Restrained character motion; no cloned voices; never commit voice recordings.
- Risk wording (funds, loans, insurance) on the relevant scene; AI-voice line in captions.
- Flag risky claims; no PRs unless asked.

At the end of each task, commit and push to GitHub.
