## Description
What if every person you met had a number floating above their head, the number of people they have been intimate with, exact, permanent and visible to everyone? This is a hypothetical thought experiment, told with stick figures. Nothing explicit is shown.

We look at who decides what counts, what surveys really say (and why the numbers are shakier than they look), what a typical number is, the double standard, dating, work, family, health, and the one thing a number can never tell you.
