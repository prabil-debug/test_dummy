Working title: What If You Became a Billionaire and No One Knew?
Status: 30-second style test, not fact-checked content (no real-world claims used).

## HOOK
You wake up and check your bank app. One billion dollars. [beat]
Nobody knows. Not your boss. Not your family. Not even the cashier who just smiled at you.

## DAY ONE
You would probably do nothing. You'd go to work. You'd eat the same sad sandwich.
Because the second you tell someone, you stop being you. You become a number.

## QUIET POWER
But quietly? You could pay off your parents' house. Fund a stranger's surgery. Buy back an hour of every single day.

## QUESTION
So here is the real question. If money changed nothing anyone could see, what would you change?
