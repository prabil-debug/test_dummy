## THE DEAL
Two weeks until the exam. And Stick is on the sofa, scrolling.
Stick has a plan: study tonight. Honestly.
Stick even made a schedule. For tomorrow.
But then Stick's brain offers a deal.
Deal one: study now, and get your reward in two weeks.
Deal two: scroll now, and get your reward right now.
Guess which one wins. [beat]
Scientists call it present bias. We overvalue now, and undervalue later.
It isn't laziness. Brains are wired to love right now.
So how do you trick your own brain?
Let's find out.
